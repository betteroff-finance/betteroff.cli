␛[1mbetteroff recurring␛[22m  List recurring

Use this when the user asks about recurring bills, subscriptions, or income. Get recurring bills,
subscriptions and income (Plaid-reported and detected streams), without credit card payments.
Duplicate streams are merged; ended streams are listed apart in `ended`. Not a payment history (use
search_transactions) or proof of insurance coverage.

␛[1mFlags␛[22m
  --coverage-days <integer>
␛[2m      Compare bills and debt minimums due in this many days with bank cash.␛[22m
