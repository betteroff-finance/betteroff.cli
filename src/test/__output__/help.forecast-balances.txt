␛[1mbetteroff forecast-balances␛[22m  Forecast balances

Use this when the user asks whether a checking or savings account will cover what is due in the next
few weeks, or how low an account will get. Project each checking and savings account's balance day
by day over the next 14 days (up to 35): the starting balance, every dated bill, card payment and
income due from that account, the running balance, and the low point with its date. Computed in code
from the account's own recurring streams; use it instead of projecting balances by hand.

␛[1mFlags␛[22m
  --days <integer>
␛[2m      How many days ahead to project. Defaults to 14.␛[22m
