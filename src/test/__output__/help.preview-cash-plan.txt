␛[1mbetteroff preview-cash-plan␛[22m  Preview cash plan

Use this when the user wants a cash-plan scenario after confirming the available cash, upcoming
obligations, spending allowance and buffer. Calculate a read-only USD cash-plan scenario from inputs
the customer explicitly confirmed. Never invent a balance, buffer, spending allowance, bill or
confirmation. Does not reserve or move money.
